# This run is a quality benchmark

The result is kept as a reference for what this studio can make at its best. For this run, these points replace the limits above:

- There is no cost limit and no 30-minute aim. The time limit is long, and the runner tells you when it is nearly used. Use what the game needs.
- Quality comes first. Everything the player looks at is finished: generated models or textures for every main thing, surroundings that make a place (ground, sky, a few 3D props), light that fits the look, a sound on every action, clear feedback for right and wrong, a result the player can read, and every level playable to its end.
- The art budget is larger; the media tools say what is left. Spend it on what the player looks at most. The picture the tools return is a reduced copy; the full-size original is in `assets/generated/originals/`. Use the original for a sky or any surface that fills the view.
- Look at the game in screenshots as often as you need, from where the player stands and close to the main things. Compare with the first-view mockup and fix what looks unfinished, empty, misplaced or hard to read.
- A game that works still comes first: keep typecheck, build and `playtest_replay` passing as you improve it, and record again any hand step your changes moved.
